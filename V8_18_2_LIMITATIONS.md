# V8.18.2 Limitations

- Official VIMAWA workbook layouts can change; diagnostics are returned for parser audit.
- A report without recognizable YTD + prior-YTD headers remains PARTIAL.
- Monthly values are DERIVED_FROM_OFFICIAL_YTD, not raw monthly disclosures.
- Older archive entries without XLSX cannot be normalized by this parser.
- Quy Nhon image-based details are not OCR'd into official records.
- AIS, tariff, SCFI/CCFI and other freight macro are intentionally outside V8.18.2.
- Raw official files are not yet archived to R2; D1 stores normalized values/provenance only.
