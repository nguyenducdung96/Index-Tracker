# V8.14 Limitations

- V8.14 focuses on information architecture, not on claiming nationwide realtime port operations.
- Only Hải Phòng has a production ship-plan collector today.
- Region cards marked SOURCE FOUND or RESEARCH intentionally show no KPI.
- The company universe contains several business models; it must not be used as an automatic valuation peer set.
- The universe will continue to expand only after official issuer/exchange verification.
- Historical throughput/capacity engines from V8.13 remain available in the code/API but are no longer top-level tabs. They will be embedded into Company/Terminal details in later versions.
