# V8.10 Limitations

- DWT is vessel design deadweight and is not cargo throughput or TEU.
- Hai Phong source is a movement plan; records are not asserted to be realized calls.
- Company aggregation covers only registered terminals.
- Ownership percentage is intentionally null until an effective-dated official registry is completed.
- YoY displays null/— until matching prior-year months exist in D1.
- GMD Nam Dinh Vu capacity is design capacity, not actual throughput.
