# V8.13 Limitations

1. There is still no single free official terminal-level actual-TEU API for all Vietnamese listed port companies.
2. GMD disclosures found for 2025 are system-wide; they are not Nam Dinh Vu actual TEU.
3. DVP 2025 actual is available, but V8.13 does not reverse-engineer the 2024 absolute TEU from the reported percentage.
4. DXP official operating throughput is disclosed in tonnes; no TEU conversion is attempted.
5. Nam Dinh Vu official sources describe Phase 3 capacity differently: the terminal equipment page lists 1.0m TEU design for Phase 3 while a corporate release says Phase 3 adds ~650k and total becomes >2m. V8.13 uses the corporate effective capacity disclosure (>2m) after 30/09/2025 and keeps the conflict documented.
6. No historical utilization is calculated unless numerator and denominator share the same asset scope and compatible period.
